# Contributing

Thanks for helping with Ant0. This guide applies to every public repository in the Ant0-Browser
organization, unless a repository has its own.

## Before you start

- For a bug, open an issue with the bug form first. Include your Ant0 version and your system.
- For a feature or a larger change, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach before you
  write the code.
- Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the repository and create a branch from `main`.
2. Keep each pull request to one change. Small ones are reviewed faster.
3. Add or update tests for what you changed, and run the repository's checks locally.
4. Update the docs or README if the behaviour changes.
5. Open the pull request and fill in the template.
6. Sign the CLA when the bot asks (first pull request only; not needed for ant0-examples).
7. If AI tools helped substantially with your change, say so in the pull request description.

Maintainers review every pull request. We may ask for changes, and we may decline a change that
doesn't fit the project. We'll say why.

## Licence and CLA

For ant0-mcp, ant0-sdk and ant0-cli, you need to accept our [Contributor Licence Agreement](https://github.com/Ant0-Browser/.github/blob/main/legal/cla-individual.md) (CLA) before we can merge your first pull request. The CLA Assistant bot comments on your pull request with a link. Sign in with GitHub, type your full name and click "I agree". This covers that pull request and all your future ones to those repositories.

You keep the copyright in your work. You license your contribution to RENEWBYTE LABS LTD, which runs Ant0, under the CLA, and we publish it under the repository's licence. The CLA also lets us use your contribution in other Ant0 products, including in closed-source products and under other licences. If we publish your contribution, we keep it available under the licence that applied to the repository on the date you submitted it.

If your employer or another organisation has rights in your contribution, don't sign the individual CLA. Your employer signs the [entity CLA](https://github.com/Ant0-Browser/.github/blob/main/legal/cla-entity.md) and lists you in it; email cla@ant0.link.

You must be 18 or over to sign the CLA. If you're under 18, you're welcome to open issues, but we can't merge your pull requests.

ant0-examples needs no CLA: contributions there are under its MIT-0 licence (see that repository's CONTRIBUTING.md).

What we store when you sign, and why: [CLA privacy notice](https://github.com/Ant0-Browser/.github/blob/main/legal/cla-privacy-notice.md).

## Conduct

Be respectful and assume good intent. Keep discussions about the code. Maintainers may remove
comments or block accounts that are abusive or off topic. Use of Ant0 itself is governed by the
[acceptable use policy](https://ant0.link/legal/acceptable-use).
